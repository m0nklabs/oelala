# DMCA Policy

**Last updated: January 5, 2026**

oelala.xyz respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects users to do the same. This policy outlines our procedures for handling copyright infringement claims under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) and equivalent EU regulations.

## 1. Reporting Copyright Infringement

If you believe content on our platform infringes your copyright, please send a written notice to our designated agent containing:

### Required Information:
1. **Your contact information**: Name, address, phone number, and email
2. **Identification of the copyrighted work**: Description or link to the original work
3. **Identification of the infringing content**: URL or description of where the infringing content appears on our Service
4. **Statement of good faith**: A statement that you have a good faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law
5. **Statement of accuracy**: A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on their behalf
6. **Signature**: Your physical or electronic signature

### Send DMCA Notices To:

**Email**: dmca@oelala.xyz  
**Subject Line**: DMCA Takedown Request

**Mail**:  
oelala.xyz DMCA Agent  
[Your business address]  
Netherlands

## 2. Our Response

Upon receiving a valid DMCA notice, we will:
1. Remove or disable access to the allegedly infringing content
2. Notify the user who posted the content
3. Provide the user an opportunity to submit a counter-notification

## 3. Counter-Notification

If you believe your content was removed in error, you may submit a counter-notification containing:

1. **Your contact information**: Name, address, phone number, and email
2. **Identification of the removed content**: Description of the content and its former location
3. **Statement of good faith**: A statement under penalty of perjury that you believe the content was removed due to mistake or misidentification
4. **Consent to jurisdiction**: A statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the courts in the Netherlands
5. **Signature**: Your physical or electronic signature

### Send Counter-Notifications To:
**Email**: dmca@oelala.xyz  
**Subject Line**: DMCA Counter-Notification

## 4. After Counter-Notification

Upon receiving a valid counter-notification, we will:
1. Forward it to the original complainant
2. Inform them they have 10-14 business days to file a court action
3. Restore the content if no court action is filed within that period

## 5. Repeat Infringer Policy

We maintain a strict repeat infringer policy:
- **First offense**: Warning and content removal
- **Second offense**: Account suspension
- **Third offense**: Permanent account termination

We reserve the right to terminate accounts immediately for egregious violations.

## 6. Misrepresentation Warning

Under the DMCA, knowingly making material misrepresentations in a takedown notice or counter-notification may result in liability for damages, including costs and attorneys' fees.

## 7. EU Users

For users in the European Union, similar procedures apply under the EU Copyright Directive (2019/790) and the Digital Services Act. The same contact information applies.

## 8. AI-Generated Content

Please note that AI-generated content may incorporate learned patterns from training data. If you believe AI-generated content infringes your specific copyrighted work, please include detailed information about your original work in your notice.
